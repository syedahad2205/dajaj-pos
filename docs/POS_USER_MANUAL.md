# POS user guide (short)

This guide is for staff who use the till (POS), not for developers.

---

## 1. How to request access

1. Open the **POS login** page (your manager gives you the link).
2. Click the **Request Access** tab.
3. Enter your **name** and **work email**, then tap **Request Access**.

After that, **wait for the admin**. They must approve your email. Until you are approved, you cannot use the POS.

**First time after approval**

1. Open the POS login page and stay on the **Login** tab.
2. Enter your **email** and choose a **password** (at least 6 characters).
3. Tap **Sign In**. The app creates your login the first time you do this after approval.

If login says *pending* or *rejected*, talk to your admin.

---

## 2. How to create a bill (charge a customer)

1. Log in to the **POS** screen.
2. Tap **New order** (or similar) to start an order.
3. Type a **customer name** or table label in the name field (required before billing).
4. Add items from the menu: choose category → item → options if shown → add to the order.
5. Change **quantity** with + / − if needed.
6. When the order is ready, tap **Bill** (or **Pay**).
7. Choose **payment type** (Cash, Card, UPI, etc.) and confirm.

The bill is saved in the system. **Sold** amounts for inventory are counted from these saved bills for each calendar day.

---

## 3. How to update inventory

Only staff who are given **inventory permission** (by the admin in staff settings) can do this.

1. Open the **Inventory** page from the POS screen (link or button your manager shows you).
2. Pick the **date** you are closing or checking (usually today).
3. For each line, enter **opening stock** and **closing stock**, then tap **Save opening** or **Save closing**. Nothing is stored until you tap those buttons.
4. **Sold** is filled automatically from bills for that date. You do not type sold yourself.

**Tips**

- Rows appear only for menu items the admin marked for tracking in **Menu builder**.
- Items marked **unavailable** in the menu are not listed for inventory.
- If a row is missing, ask the admin to turn on tracking for that menu category or item.

---

## Questions?

Ask your **admin** or **manager**. They control access, menu, and who can edit inventory.
